Your documents and responsibilities

You remain responsible for documents you scan, import, edit, save, share or print with GregyScan.

Use GregyScan only with documents and information you are legally allowed to process.

Before relying on or sending a document, you should review:

  • all scanned pages
  • cropping
  • rotation
  • filters and cleanup results
  • redactions
  • signatures and stamps
  • filenames
  • storage destinations
  • shared attachments

GregyScan is a document-processing convenience tool.

It does not independently verify:

  • document authenticity
  • identity
  • legal validity
  • accuracy of document content
  • regulatory compliance
  • successful delivery
  • acceptance by a recipient

Keep original documents when accuracy, authenticity or evidentiary value matters.

Visual signatures and stamps

Signatures and stamps added through GregyScan are visual image annotations.

They are not cryptographic digital signatures and do not prove:

  • identity
  • authorization
  • document authenticity
  • document integrity
  • non-repudiation

Users are responsible for determining whether a visual signature or stamp is appropriate for their intended purpose.

Storage and backups

Recent scans are temporary working copies and are not intended to serve as a permanent document archive.

Android may remove temporary application data.

Users are responsible for confirming that important PDFs or images have been saved to a permanent location and for maintaining any backups they require.

Files stored in shared storage, Gallery, a user-selected folder or an external provider may remain after GregyScan is uninstalled.

Cleanup or redaction operations may change document pixels and may remove or alter information.

Keep an original copy when preservation of the original document matters.
