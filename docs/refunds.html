Refund information

Last updated: 2026-09-30

GregyScan is a free and open-source Android application.

The current public GregyScan release does not include:

  • paid application purchases
  • subscriptions
  • in-app purchases
  • Premium features
  • paid feature unlocking
  • advertising purchases

Because GregyScan does not sell digital products or application features, there is no GregyScan application purchase to refund.

Optional support

Users may optionally support GregyScan development through Buy Me a Coffee:

https://buymeacoffee.com/gregyscan

Contributions are voluntary support for continued development.

A contribution does not:

  • purchase GregyScan
  • unlock features
  • create a subscription
  • provide Premium access
  • change support priority

Contribution refunds

Payments made through Buy Me a Coffee are processed by that service and its payment providers.

If you need help with a contribution or want to ask about a refund, please use the payment or support options provided by Buy Me a Coffee and the payment provider shown on your receipt.

Any mandatory rights provided by applicable law remain unaffected.
